# Features

Capabilities are grouped by who uses them. This is a product inventory, not an implementation guide.

## Shopper experience

- Home merchandising: sliders, banners, featured collections, brands, and site feature blocks
- Category and subcategory browsing, brand pages, and search
- Product detail with images, optional video, attributes, pricing, ratings, and related products
- Compare list and wishlist
- Follow a seller store
- Cart, buy-now, shipping selection, and voucher validation
- Guest checkout (when enabled) and registered checkout
- Multiple payment methods, including cash on delivery and bank transfer
- Order history, order tracking, and cancellation requests
- Reviews and review images
- Saved addresses and profile management
- Newsletter subscription and contact form
- Store pages with optional WhatsApp contact

## Seller / vendor

- Seller registration and verification
- Store profile: branding, SEO metadata, and storefront presence
- Catalog ownership: products, variants, images, and inventory
- Order handling for the seller’s own sales
- Commission-aware earnings
- Withdrawal accounts and payout requests (platform-approved)
- POS sales against the seller’s catalog (when the POS module is enabled)

Sellers do not administer platform-wide settings such as global CMS, tax rules, shipping rules, or user management.

## Platform administration

- Dashboard and order statistics
- User and guest-user management
- Role and permission management for admin staff
- Categories, subcategories, brands, tags, and product attributes
- Full product lifecycle, including multi-image upload and video
- Variant inventory (SKU, quantity, price) and image-to-attribute mapping
- Tax rules and shipping rules (location or pickup)
- Flash sales, vouchers, bundle deals, and product collections
- Home sliders and banners with configurable link targets (category, product, brand, URL)
- CMS pages, header/footer links, and site features
- Site settings, logos, currency, address, analytics, and miscellaneous flags
- Payment gateway configuration (keys are stored privately in production; not shown in this showcase)
- Media storage configuration (local, cloud object storage, or CDN URL)
- SMTP and social-login configuration
- Custom scripts (for approved third-party snippets)
- Newsletter formats and subscriber messaging
- Contact-message inbox
- Language management
- Bulk product import and export
- Plugin installation and activation (for example, POS)
- Cache maintenance for operators

## Point of sale

- POS settings (receipt/branding configuration)
- Counter orders recorded as first-class orders
- Cash, cash-on-delivery, and offline POS payment methods
- Shared inventory impact with online sales
- POS order history in the admin console

## Commerce and operations

- Order statuses from pending through delivered
- Payment status and payment-method updates
- Delivered-order email
- Cancellation and refund handling
- Guest vs. registered customer orders
- Multi-currency display settings (symbol, position, decimal format)

## Localization and content

- Storefront and admin UI languages: English, French, Arabic (RTL), Turkish, Hindi
- Translated catalog entities (products, categories, brands, CMS, and related records)
- Per-page and per-product SEO fields, including meta keywords
- Right-to-left layout support for Arabic

## Trust, growth, and engagement

- Ratings and reviews
- Email verification and password recovery
- Google and Facebook sign-in
- Optional Google Analytics
- Promotional email formats
